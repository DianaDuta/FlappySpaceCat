Privacy Policy for Flappy Space Cat

Effective Date: October 2026

Developer: Dianarcado

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Flappy Space Cat (the "App"), developed and published by Dianarcado. We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

When you play Flappy Space Cat, we may collect and process the following information:

  • Account Information: If you register or log in via Email or Google Sign-In, we collect your unique user identifier (UID) and email address through Firebase Authentication.
  • Game Progress & Cloud Save: In-game high scores, gems collected, unlocked skins, and completed achievements stored via Cloud Firestore.
  • Device & Usage Data: Anonymized analytical data, including device model, operating system version, session duration, and gameplay telemetry collected via Firebase Analytics to help improve app performance and stability.
  • Advertising Data: Non-personally identifiable advertising identifiers to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads via Google AdMob.

2. Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify you according to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Games
  • Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Analytics)
  • Google AdMob

3. In-App Purchases

Any digital purchases (such as gem packs or skin unlocks) are securely processed directly by Google Play In-App Billing. We do not store or process your credit card or financial details.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Flappy Space Cat is designed as a casual arcade game for all audiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such data.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your cloud save data for as long as you maintain an account. You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated game data at any time by contacting us at the email provided below.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any changes.
